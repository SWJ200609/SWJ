① 通用功能句（CET-6 + 考研英语二 通用）

开头段（引出话题/引出图片）

🔹 As is vividly illustrated in the picture, ...　图中生动展现了……
🔹 The drawing above depicts ...　上图描绘了……
🔹 According to the chart/passage, ...　根据图表/文章……
🔹 In recent years, there has been a growing tendency that ...　近年来出现……的趋势
🔹 The topic of ... has recently aroused widespread concern.　……话题已引起广泛关注的
🔹 Few things are more important than ...　没有比……更重要的了
🔹 Nowadays, ... plays an increasingly significant role in ...　如今，……在……中的作用越来越重要

衔接/过渡（段与段之间）

🔹 表观点：From my perspective / In my view / Personally, ...
🔹 表赞同：It is well known that ... / There is no denying that ...
🔹 表转折：However / Nevertheless / Conversely / Yet, ...
🔹 表递进：What is more / Moreover / Furthermore / Not only ..., but also ...
🔹 表因果：Therefore / Consequently / As a result / Thus, ...
🔹 表举例：For instance / To illustrate / A case in point is that ...

结论段（总结/建议/展望）

🔹 Taking all these factors into account, we may reasonably arrive at the conclusion that ...　综合上述因素，可合理得出结论：……
🔹 In summary / To sum up, ...　总而言之
🔹 It is high time that we took effective measures to ...　是时候采取有效措施了
🔹 Only in this way can we ensure ...　唯有如此才能确保
🔹 Individual efforts alone are far from enough; ... also requires ...　仅靠个人努力远远不够，还需要……
🔹 The day is not far away when ...　……的日子已不远了

③ 翻译高频表达库（考研英语二 Part IV）

经济类

🔹 economic growth 经济增长　economic recovery 经济复苏　economic downturn 经济下行
🔹 market economy 市场经济　supply and demand 供给与需求　inflation / deflation 通胀/通缩
🔹 investment 投资　consumption 消费　export / import 出口/进口
🔹 the gross domestic product (GDP) 国内生产总值
🔹 to boost / promote / stimulate economic development 促进经济发展

社会类

🔹 social inequality 社会不平等　social mobility 社会流动
🔹 social welfare 社会福利　the underprivileged 弱势群体
🔹 poverty alleviation 扶贫　to bridge the gap between ... and ... 缩小……差距
🔹 public order 公共秩序　civic awareness 公民意识
🔹 to play an increasingly important role in society 在社会中扮演越来越重要的角色

科技/信息类

🔹 information technology 信息技术　artificial intelligence (AI) 人工智能
🔹 data 数据　information overload 信息过载
🔹 online shopping 网购　social media 社交媒体　the digital age 数字时代
🔹 to have a profound impact on 对……有深远影响
🔹 to bring about a revolution in 给……带来革命

环境类

🔹 environmental protection 环境保护　global warming 全球变暖　carbon emission 碳排放
🔹 sustainable development 可持续发展　renewable energy 可再生能源
🔹 to reduce pollution 减少污染　to protect biodiversity 保护生物多样性
🔹 to raise environmental awareness 提高环保意识

万能结构（翻译时反复用）

🔹 There is a widespread belief that ...　普遍认为……
🔹 It is increasingly recognized that ...　人们日益认识到……
🔹 According to a recent report, ...　根据最近的一份报告
🔹 The significance of ... lies in the fact that ...　……的重要性在于……
🔹 ... is of great significance to / plays a key role in　……对……意义重大 / 起关键作用

④ 图表/图画作文常用

描述图表数据（英语二小作文常见）

🔹 The bar/line/pie chart illustrates ...　柱状图/折线图/饼图显示了……
🔹 The figures have been on the rise since ...　自……以来数据一直呈上升态势
🔹 It accounts for / takes up a large proportion of ...　占比很大
🔹 It doubled / tripled / fell by / rose by X percent.
🔹 Compared with 2010, it increased by a sharp 30%.
🔹 There was a dramatic/slight/gradual change in ...　……发生了剧烈/细微/逐渐的变化

描述图画场景（英语一大作文 + 英语二部分年份）

🔹 In the drawing, we can see ...　图中我们可以看到……
🔹 On the left/right side, there is ...　左侧/右侧有一……
🔹 At the bottom of the picture, the caption reads: "..."　图下方标题写着
🔹 The characters in the picture are shown to ...　图中人物正在……
🔹 From the picture, one can draw the conclusion that ...　从图中可以得出……结论

英语二小作文格式铁律 ① 通知：首行居中 NOTICE，右上角日期，最后一行右对齐落款；正文三段
② 邀请信：Dear + 称呼, + 三段 + Sincerely yours, ...，首行缩进；称呼独占一行，逗号紧跟称呼后，不能另起行
③ 建议信 / 感谢信：同上格式
④ 道歉信：同上格式，正文第一段说明原因，第二段致歉，第三段补救方案
⑤ 称呼后必须是逗号（不是冒号），正文每段首行缩进，落款独占行
