📐 语法与句式

9 大考点 · 阅读解长难句 + 写作升格的核心
长难句 非谓语 倒装 强调 虚拟语气 独立主格 主谓一致 定语从句 名词化

① 长难句三步切分法（阅读解句核心）

  1. 找谓语动词——句子的骨干；主谓宾先定，其它都是修饰
  2. 找连接词——that / which / who / when / although / because / if / so that 后面是修饰
  3. 找非谓语——-ing / -ed / to do 都是修饰语，剥掉不影响主干
真题示范
While the Internet has transformed how people communicate, it has not, as some have claimed, replaced the need for face-to-face interaction.
① 主谓宾：it（主）+ has not replaced（谓）+ the need（宾）
② 而 While... 是从属，as some have claimed 是插入的让步状语从句
③ 直译：「尽管互联网彻底改变了人们交流的方式，但它并不能像一些人所说的那样取代面对面交流的必要性。」

② 非谓语动词（三种形式，功能固定）

形式含义典型用法真题例句
to do（不定式）将来/目的/具体目的状语 · 形容词后 · 固定搭配（be ready to / be able to / would like to）The purpose of this measure is to reduce energy consumption.
-ing（现在分词）主动/正在状语 · 定语 · 宾语补足语The report, published last year, has been widely criticized.（-ed 表被动）
Faced with rising costs, the company had no choice.（-ed 作原因状语）
-ed（过去分词）被动/完成定语 · 状语 · 形容词化（satisfied / interested / exhausted）Customers bothered by noise complaints are unlikely to return.
三大易错 ① 逻辑主语不一致：句子主语必须和非谓语动词的逻辑主语一致，否则用独立主格（见下）。
② be to do / be about to do 表示「即将」；be due to do 也是「将要」。
③ have sth. done 使动结构（=请人做）：I had my car repaired. = 我让人修好了车。

③ 倒装（写作加分神器）

完全倒装（地点/时间/方式状语 + 谓语 + 主语）

🔹 Here comes the bus.
🔹 On the hill stands an old temple.
🔹 Out rushed the children.

部分倒装（否定/限制副词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语）

🔹 In no case should we ignore...
🔹 Never have I seen...
🔹 Only in this way can we solve the problem.
🔹 Not until 1990 did the government admit...
🔹 Not only does it save money, but it also saves time.（后半句不倒装）

④ 强调句（It is/was ... that/who ...）

🔹 模板：It is [强调部分] that/who [剩余部分].
🔹 It was the government that made the decision.
🔹 It is in this way that we can learn effectively.
🔹 提问形式：Was it John who stole the car?

判断是不是强调句：把 It is ... that 去掉，剩下的还能组成完整句——是，就是强调句。

⑤ 虚拟语气四类（翻译写作高频）

情况结构例句
与现在相反if + 过去时 / would doIf I were you, I would apply for the job.
与过去相反if + 过去完成 / would have doneHad it not been for the rain, we would have gone.（省 if 用 Had）
与将来相反if + 过去时/were to/could do / would doIf he were to accept, I would go with him.
含蓄虚拟as if/as though/would rather/otherwise/but forHe talks as if he knew everything.
I would rather you stayed here.

⑥ 独立主格（高级书面表达）

结构：名词/代词 + 非谓语(或介词短语)，作状语。
🔹 Weather permitting, we will leave tomorrow.
🔹 His homework done, he went out to play.
🔹 There being no bus, we had to walk.

⑦ 主谓一致（翻译语法题必考）

就近原则（There be / here be / or 连接）

🔹 There is a book and two pens on the desk.
🔹 Either you or they are wrong.（主语是 they 就用 are）

就远原则（and 连接的两个并列主语 → 复数）

🔹 Tom and Jerry are my friends.

整体观念（看作一体 → 单数）

🔹 集合名词：family / team / class / crowd / audience / government / committee 强调整体 → 单数，强调成员 → 复数。
🔹 复合名词：news / mathematics / physics / politics / furniture / equipment 一律单数。
🔹 不可数：water / information / advice / knowledge 单数。
🔹 距离/时间/金钱：Three thousand yuan is enough.
🔹 The number of... is（数目）vs A number of... are（许多）

⑧ 定语从句关系词（一表就够）

先行词关系词备注
指人who / that / which 主宾 · whom 宾 · whose 所有格口语中 that 常代替 whom
指物which / that 主宾 · whose 所有格介词后只能用 which/that
地点where = in/at/on whichthe city where
时间when = on/at/in whichthe day when
原因why = for whichthe reason why
介词后只能用 which/whom，不可用 thatthe house in which
限制性 vs 非限制性 非限制性（用逗号隔开）：只能用 which / who / whom，不能用 that。 例：The Internet, which has changed our lives, is indispensable. ✓
... that has changed our lives, ... ✗

⑨ 名词化（学术语体 + 翻译高频）

把动词/形容词变名词，是学术英文的核心特征——翻译题常考的「地道化」手段。
🔹 reduce → reduction：The reduction of carbon emissions is essential.
🔹 develop → development：rapid economic development
🔹 compete → competition：fierce market competition
🔹 decide → decision：make an important decision
🔹 protect → protection：legal protection of rights
🔹 participate → participation：active participation in social activities

写作升格口诀「动词变名词，句子更正式」。把 We should protect the environment. 改成 Environmental protection should be given priority.，学术味立刻上升。
