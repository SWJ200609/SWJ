🔗 高频固定搭配

完形/翻译/写作三处通用 · 8 组核心

① be 类固定搭配

🔹 be absorbed in 全神贯注于
🔹 be attached to 依恋；附属
🔹 be committed to (doing) 致力于
🔹 be detrimental to 对……有害
🔹 be engaged in 从事于
🔹 be entitled to 有权做
🔹 be ignorant of 对……无知
🔹 be involved in 卷入、参与
🔹 be inferior to 劣于
🔹 be superior to 优于
🔹 be responsible for 对……负责
🔹 be subject to 服从于；易于

② 动词 + 介词（方向易错）

🔹 contribute to doing 促成、导致
🔹 substitute A for B（用 A 代替 B） ↔ replace B with A（用 A 替换 B）
🔹 attribute A to B 把 A 归因于 B ↔ attribute to 归因于（被动）
🔹 consist of / be composed of 由……组成
🔹 account for 解释；占（比例）
🔹 result in 导致（结果） ↔ result from 由……引起
🔹 devote … to 把……献给
🔹 object to doing 反对做
🔹 persist in 坚持
🔹 participate in 参与
🔹 compete with / for / in 与……竞争／争夺／在……中

③ 倒装与固定表达（写作加分项）

🔹 in no circumstances / on no account / at no time + 倒装：In no circumstances should we give up.
🔹 Only by / only in this way + 倒装：Only in this way can we achieve success.
🔹 Not only … but also …（前半句倒装）：Not only did he succeed, but he also taught others.
🔹 So / Neither + 倒装：He passed the exam, and so did I.
🔹 Hardly … when …：Hardly had he arrived when the meeting began.
🔹 Scarcely … when …：Scarcely had I sat down when the phone rang.

④ 名词 + 介词（搭配记忆）

🔹 the key to 解决问题的关键
🔹 the cause of 原因
🔹 the effect of 影响
🔹 the advantage of 优势
🔹 in favour of 赞成（英式）↔ in favor of（美式）
🔹 in terms of 就……而言
🔹 by virtue of 由于、凭借
🔹 for the sake of 为了……的利益
🔹 at the expense of 以……为代价
🔹 on the basis of 在……的基础上

最高频陷阱 substitute A for B 和 replace B with A —— 介词方向相反，考场上如果记不清，两个都用错，一个用对一个错。
attribute A to B —— to 是介词，不是不定式，后面接名词或动名词。
